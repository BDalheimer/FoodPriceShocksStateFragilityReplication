\begin{tablenotes}\small
\item \textit{Notes:} Within-group Rotemberg weight decomposition following \citet{goldsmith2020bartik}. For each group-specific local projection (outcome: $\Delta$FSI; regressor: group consumption shock $B^g_{it}$), the within-group weight $\hat{\alpha}_c^g = \text{Cov}(\tilde{B}_c, \tilde{B}_g) / \text{Var}(\tilde{B}_g)$ measures the contribution of commodity $c$ to identification within group $g$. Residualisation ($\tilde{\cdot}$) removes year fixed effects and two lags of the group shock. HHI $= \sum_c (\hat{\alpha}_c^g)^2$; effective $N = 1/$HHI. The bottom row reproduces the aggregate-model HHI for reference. Effective $N > 1$ indicates identification is not fully concentrated in a single commodity.
